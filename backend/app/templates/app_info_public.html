Atta Video AI

Atta Video AI is an AI-assisted video production and YouTube publishing application. Its purpose is to help users create, manage, render and publish complete video projects from one production workflow.

What Atta Video AI Does

  • Generates and organizes video scripts.
  • Creates narration and scene plans.
  • Sources or generates media for video scenes.
  • Creates captions and video assets.
  • Renders complete videos.
  • Manages video production and publishing queues.
  • Connects an authorized YouTube channel through Google OAuth.
  • Uploads completed videos to a channel selected by the authorized user.

YouTube and Google OAuth

Atta Video AI allows users to connect their own Google and YouTube accounts through Google OAuth. Google authorization is used only to provide the YouTube functionality requested by the account owner.

Why Google User Data Is Requested

The YouTube read-only permission is used to identify and display information about the YouTube channel that the user chooses to connect.

The YouTube upload permission is used to upload a completed video to that authorized channel when the user requests or configures a publishing action.

Atta Video AI does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell Google user data, and does not use Google user data to train artificial-intelligence models.

User Control

YouTube channels remain under the control of their respective Google account owners. A connected channel can be disconnected when access is no longer required, and access can also be revoked through the user's Google Account.
